How does shipping work across Canada, and how do I get Free Shipping?

We ship nationwide to all Canadian provinces and territories using secure, tracked shipping via Canada Post and express couriers. All orders over $75 qualify for 100% Free Shipping. In-stock orders placed before 2:00 PM MST are dispatched within 24 hours with email tracking.

What is your return policy for online orders?

We want you to be thrilled with your machine. We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy on brand-new, unused vacuums in original manufacturer packaging. If you need setup help or technical guidance, our certified team is only a phone call away.

Are all products authentic and covered by full Canadian factory warranties?

Yes. Dynamic Vacuums is an Authorized Canadian Dealer & Warranty Depot for Dyson, Miele, SEBO, Duovac, Beam, Nilfisk, and Sanitaire. Every vacuum you order online is 100% brand-new, factory-sealed, and eligible for complete Canadian manufacturer warranty coverage.

How does the 100% Price Match Guarantee work online?

If you find an identical, in-stock item advertised at a lower price by any authorized Canadian dealer, we will match it on the spot. Simply send us the product link or call our toll-free line before or at checkout.

Can I buy online and pick up at your Calgary showroom?

Yes! During online checkout, choose "Free In-Store Pickup". We will prepare and inspect your vacuum so it is ready for same-day or next-day collection at our Calgary showroom: 165 – 10233 Elbow Dr S.W.

How do I find the exact replacement bags, filters, or parts for my model?

We stock one of Western Canada's largest inventories of genuine OEM filters, HEPA bags, belts, and powerhead assemblies. You can search by your machine's model number online or send a photo of your vacuum's serial plate to info@dynamicvac.com for instant matching.

What secure payment methods are accepted on your store?
